# 格致 Logo 三方向概念板

用途：供用户比较品牌方向，尚未替换软件正式图标。

生成方式：内置 Image 生成工具。原图复制到同目录 `logo-directions-v3-20261006.png`，保持原始字节，没有裁切或编辑。

设计判断：优先考虑 03 GEZHI SEAL。方形构件呼应“格”，负形 Z 对应 GEZHI，在软件图标和发布片片尾均有明确轮廓。01 更直接对应六维；02 更强调对话关系。

## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one premium brand-direction comparison board, landscape, for GEZHI, an educational desktop application centered on digital learning spaces and evidence-based six-dimensional explanation feedback. This is brainstorming artwork, not installed production assets.
Primary request: create three highly refined, clearly DISTINCT new geometric logo concepts on a single editorial presentation board. Restrained international technology brand quality, sharp vector-like silhouettes, beautiful optical balance, exceptional clarity at small icon size.
Composition: three equal columns with generous whitespace. Upper two thirds on warm white, lower third on deep graphite. Each column has one large BLACK mark on white, the exact same mark repeated in WHITE on graphite, and a small lavender rounded app-icon thumbnail near the bottom. Consistent mark scale and consistent alignment across columns. Tiny pale-gray separators only. At top left a small tasteful wordmark GEZHI. Exact column labels underneath large marks: "01  INSIGHT WINDOW", "02  DIALOGUE GATE", "03  GEZHI SEAL". Small exact descriptor under each respectively: "Space / Six dimensions", "Dialogue / Perspective", "Structure / Understanding". Clean sans-serif typography, all text English.
Concept 01: six substantial rounded geometric segments form an open hexagonal portal, with a purposeful entrance and a simple diagonal interior passage subtly suggesting the letter Z. Architecture of a learning space, six viewpoints, confident compact outline; carefully simplify so the mark is not busy. Bold enough for a 24-pixel favicon.
Concept 02: two facing abstract quotation / bracket forms compose an inviting squared gateway. The positive and negative space imply a conversation and an inward path. Slightly asymmetric, elegant, warm, visually lighter than concept 03. No interwoven loops.
Concept 03: a compact square seal built from four asymmetric rounded geometric modules, with a crisp Z-shaped negative-space corridor across the center. The grid evokes the Chinese name GEZHI; the path evokes moving from fragments to understanding. Strong, memorable silhouette.
Style constraints: flat black and white master symbols, gentle corner radii, precise controlled stroke weight, no 3D mockup gimmicks, no glossy metal, no shadows behind main marks, no gradients in master marks, no book / graduation-cap / brain / robot clichés, no knots, no petal rosettes, no resemblance to OpenAI or Gemini trademarks. Thumbnail accent color is restrained lavender #a38be8. Clean immaculate contemporary Swiss-style brand design sheet. Do not add explanatory paragraphs or extra logos.
